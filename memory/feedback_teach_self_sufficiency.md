When the user asks something they could verify themselves (e.g. "am I on main?", "did that work?"), answer the question but also show them the exact command or step to check it themselves.

**Why:** User wants to build self-sufficiency, not just get answers handed to them. Fits their learning style of understanding HOW, not just getting results.

**How to apply:** After answering, add a short "To check yourself:" note with the relevant command or action.
