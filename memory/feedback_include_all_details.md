When describing what to write (config, code, structure), include every detail — even things that seem obvious or minor, like wrapper objects, required syntax, or JSON structure.

**Why:** User is still learning. Small omissions cause mysterious errors and slow them down. What seems minor to an expert is often a blocker for a learner.

**How to apply:** Before the user writes anything themselves, walk through the complete structure including all required wrappers, syntax, and formatting. Don't summarize or shorthand — be explicit.
